WHO WE ARE

HIDAH Humanitarian Foundation is a humanitarian and community-focused organisation committed to empowering lives, serving humanity, and transforming communities. We believe that every individual deserves the opportunity to live with dignity, hope, and purpose, regardless of their background or circumstances. Our work focuses on creating meaningful impact through healthcare, education, skills development, women and youth empowerment, and humanitarian support for vulnerable individuals and communities. We organise initiatives such as free health screenings, skills training programmes, educational support, and assistance for widows, orphans, and less privileged families.

Explore our programs →

Identity

At HIDAH, we believe that true service is not only about giving—it is about equipping people with the knowledge, skills, confidence, and opportunities they need to become self-reliant and contribute positively to society. Through partnerships with individuals, institutions, professionals, businesses, and community leaders, HIDAH Humanitarian Foundation continues to build bridges of hope and create sustainable opportunities for those who need them most.

Vision

To build resilient, empowered, and inclusive communities where every individual has access to opportunities, dignity, and hope, enabling them to thrive and contribute to sustainable development.Our vision is to see stronger individuals, empowered families, and transformed communities, where compassion is turned into action and every helping hand becomes a source of hope.

Mission

To empowering vulnerable individuals and communities through quality education, healthcare, humanitarian relief, women and youth empowerment, child protection, livelihood support, and sustainable community development. We work in partnership with communities and stakeholders to promote compassion, equity, and lasting positive change for all.

Our Programs

Creating lasting impact through sustainable initiatives

Health

HIDAH believes that access to basic healthcare is a fundamental part of human dignity. We therefore work to bring essential health services closer to underserved and vulnerable communities. Our interventions span free screenings, medical consultations, referrals, health education and more — reaching those who need it most.

Education

HIDAH believes that education is one of the strongest tools for breaking the cycle of poverty and creating sustainable opportunities. Our initiatives focus on giving people practical knowledge and skills that improve their personal, professional and economic lives through vocational training and more.

Empowerment

HIDAH sees young people as leaders, entrepreneurs, innovators and future change-makers. Our youth empowerment programs give young people the knowledge, confidence and practical skills needed to become economically independent and contribute positively to their communities.

Health

HIDAH believes that access to basic healthcare is a fundamental part of human dignity. We therefore work to bring essential health services closer to underserved and vulnerable communities.

Our health interventions include:

  • Free community health screenings
  • General medical consultations and examinations
  • Blood pressure and blood sugar checks
  • Malaria testing and screening
  • Laboratory investigations
  • Eye screening and basic eye care
  • Dental screening and oral health education
  • Gynecological and women's health screening
  • Maternal and reproductive health education
  • Health education and disease-prevention awareness
  • Menstrual hygiene education and distribution of sanitary pads
  • Medication support for people who cannot afford treatment
  • NHIS registration, renewal and education
  • Health referrals for individuals requiring further medical attention
  • Community health education on prevention and healthy living
  • Collaboration with hospitals, clinics, doctors, nurses, midwives, laboratories and other healthcare professionals

HIDAH also uses health outreaches as an opportunity to identify health conditions early and connect beneficiaries to appropriate healthcare services.

Education & Skills Training

HIDAH believes that education is one of the strongest tools for breaking the cycle of poverty and creating sustainable opportunities. Our education and training initiatives focus on giving people practical knowledge and skills that can improve their personal, professional and economic lives.

Our activities include:

  • Vocational and practical skills training
  • Event decoration and creative decoration training
  • Event planning
  • Entrepreneurship education
  • Business development training
  • Digital skills and technology education
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI) awareness and practical training
  • Leadership development
  • Public speaking and communication skills
  • Personal development
  • Career development and mentorship
  • Training on turning skills into sustainable businesses
  • Educational support for vulnerable children
  • Provision of learning materials where possible
  • School-based educational programs
  • Health and hygiene education in schools
  • Menstrual hygiene education for girls

Our recent HIDAH 2-Day Skills Development Program is an example of this approach, where participants were equipped with practical skills in decoration and event planning alongside leadership, entrepreneurship and AI-related knowledge.

Youth Empowerment

HIDAH sees young people as leaders, entrepreneurs, innovators and future change-makers. Our youth empowerment programs are designed not only to give young people knowledge, but to help them develop the confidence and practical skills needed to become economically independent and contribute positively to their communities.

We focus on:

  • Entrepreneurship and business development
  • Vocational and income-generating skills
  • Leadership training
  • Public speaking and communication
  • Career guidance
  • Mentorship
  • Digital literacy
  • AI tools and technology
  • Creativity and innovation
  • Event planning and creative industries
  • Financial and business awareness
  • Turning talents and skills into businesses
  • Building confidence and self-esteem
  • Connecting young people to opportunities and mentors
  • Encouraging young people to become community leaders and volunteers

Our goal is to move young people from dependency to opportunity, from skills to income, and from potential to leadership.

Orphans, Widows & The Less Privileged

At the heart of HIDAH's humanitarian work is our commitment to people who are vulnerable, disadvantaged or struggling to meet their basic needs. We support orphans and vulnerable children, widows, less privileged families, vulnerable women and girls, needy individuals, and people facing economic hardship.

Food & Essential Support

  • Food packages
  • Ramadan food distributions
  • Eid food support
  • Qurbani / Eid-ul-Adha distributions
  • Support for vulnerable families during special occasions
  • Essential household items where resources permit

Support for Children & Women

  • Eid clothing for orphans and vulnerable children
  • Support for widows
  • Children's welfare support
  • Menstrual hygiene products for girls
  • School-related support where possible

Educational Support

  • Learning materials
  • Educational assistance for vulnerable children
  • Support to orphanages and schools
  • Educational and personal development activities

Humanitarian Assistance

  • Responding to identified community needs
  • Supporting families facing hardship
  • Community donations
  • Emergency and welfare assistance where resources allow
  • Connecting vulnerable people to available health and social services

Our objective is not simply to provide temporary relief, but wherever possible to combine humanitarian assistance with education, healthcare, skills development and empowerment — helping beneficiaries move toward greater dignity and self-reliance.
