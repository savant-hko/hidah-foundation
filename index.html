Our mission

At HIDAH, we believe every person deserves access to education, healthcare, and dignity. Founded in 2015, we’ve partnered with local leaders in 12 countries to build sustainable programs that break the cycle of poverty.

From building schools in rural areas to providing medical supplies and vocational training, we work hand-in-hand with communities to create lasting change.

Our Programs

Creating lasting impact through sustainable initiatives

Health

Access to quality healthcare is a fundamental right. We provide mobile clinics, maternal care, health education, and medical supplies to underserved communities, ensuring no one is left behind.

Education

Education is the catalyst for social transformation. We build schools, train teachers, provide scholarships, and ensure every child has equal access to quality learning and knowledge to unlock their full potential.

Orphanage & Needs

Every child deserves a loving home and a safe environment. We support orphanages, provide basic needs like food, clothing, and shelter, and create nurturing spaces where vulnerable children can thrive and grow.
